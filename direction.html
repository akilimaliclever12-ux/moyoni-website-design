The idea

In the heart

“Moyoni” means in the heart. The site feels personal and unhurried: space, warm light, real people in rich fabrics.

Dignity, not noise

Modest, high-end, confident. No flashing badges, no animated backgrounds. Photography does the talking.

Culture as a signature

The Tanzanian pattern appears as bands, tags and dividers, the way it does on packaging and hang-tags.

Fast by design

Still images, two font families, tiny vector patterns. Every choice is checked against a speed budget (below).

Speed budget · every screen is held to this

Hero image — one still photo, about 100 KB, modern format. No autoplay video on phones.

Fonts — two families, three files (Playfair 500 and 600, Lato 400 and 700), self-hosted.

Patterns — small vector tiles, a few KB. Never an animated background.

Motion — simple fades only, off for reduced-motion users.

Images — real sizes set, lazy below the fold, product photos about 600 px wide on phones.

Scripts — Shopify, cart and one analytics tag. Anything else needs a reason.

Targets (not yet measured)

Mobile PageSpeed 90 or more, largest paint under 2.5 s, no layout jumps. Your current store already scores 92 mobile and 98 desktop, so this is the bar to keep.
